# roozTrans — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

roozTrans is built to keep your data on your phone. **The developer does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.** There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.

## What stays on your phone
- **Text you type, photos you choose, and screenshots** are processed on the phone. Screenshots taken by the screen translator are kept only in memory long enough to read the text, then discarded. They are never saved or uploaded.
- **Settings and API keys** are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them. (Android's own device backup may include app settings if you have backup turned on.)

## When data leaves your phone
Only in these cases:

| What | Sent to | When |
|---|---|---|
| The **recognised or typed text** to translate (not images) | The AI provider you chose: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, or DeepSeek | Only if you have turned on online translation **and** entered your own API key for that provider |
| A request to find your **country from your IP address** (no other data) | ipinfo.io, api.country.is, ipapi.co, myip.ipip.net | Only once you have added an API key (or tap "Check network" in Settings), to choose a provider that works in your country (for example, DeepSeek in mainland China) |
| Offline language-pack download, plus limited diagnostic and usage data collected by Google's ML Kit library | Google | When you download the offline packs, and as described in [Google's ML Kit terms](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms) |
| An update check (no personal data) | The developer's website | **Direct-download version only.** The Google Play version is updated by Google Play |

Each of these services receives your IP address as part of any internet connection, and handles data under its own privacy policy:
[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) ·
[OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy) ·
[DeepSeek](https://platform.deepseek.com/downloads/DeepSeek%20Privacy%20Policy.html) ·
[Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
Do not translate sensitive personal information online if you are not comfortable sending it to the provider you chose. Offline mode sends nothing.

## Accessibility service
roozTrans uses Android's Accessibility API **only** to:
1. show the floating translate bubble over other apps;
2. take a screenshot when **you** tap the bubble or the Quick Settings tile, or double-press Volume Down;
3. detect that Volume-Down double-press. No other key presses are read or recorded.

It does **not** read screen content in the background, does not collect personal data through Accessibility, and does not perform actions in other apps. The app asks for your consent before you enable it, and you can turn it off at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility.

## Permissions
- **Internet / network state:** online translation, country lookup, and update checks (direct version only).
- **No camera, storage, contacts or location permission.** The camera button opens your phone's own camera app, and the gallery button uses Android's photo picker, so roozTrans sees only the photo you choose.
- **Install packages** (direct-download version only): install its own updates.
- **Ignore battery optimisations** (direct-download version only): lets you exempt roozTrans from the battery saver in one tap, so the phone does not switch the screen translator off while it sleeps. The Google Play version opens the battery settings instead.

## Children
roozTrans is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
